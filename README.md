## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The trainer does not alter basic character movement. Navigate normally through the game's activity scenes and interact with the objectives presented for each fairy.

### Tools & Inventory

My Sexy Fairies does not use a traditional RPG inventory system. The trainer instead focuses on mission progress, activity completion, relationship advancement, and rewards earned during the story.

### World & Environment

Progress through the atmospheric fantasy setting by helping four fairies with their personal challenges. The blue fairy works with magical stones, the green fairy restores an ancient library, the red fairy conducts potion experiments, and the golden fairy practices combat.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is presented as part of the golden fairy's training activities rather than as a large standalone combat campaign. Activate combat assistance only during the relevant training sequence.
